Privacy Policy

NAS Backup is a private tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google Drive to upload, list, download and delete backup files.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to store and restore the owner's backups. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

Backups stay in the owner's Google Drive. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the project issue page.
